Affiliate disclosure

Some pages on this site include a small box headed Tours & things to do nearby. The links in that box are affiliate links. If you click one and then book, we may earn a commission. You pay the same price as you would going to the site directly.

Active affiliate programs

  • Viator (viator.com, tours and activities marketplace).
  • Guide to Iceland (guidetoiceland.is, Icelandic tours and travel booking platform).

These are the only affiliate programs active on this site. We have no affiliate or referral arrangement with any campsite, and none with any campervan or motorhome rental company.

What this means for you

  • No extra cost. Booking through one of our links does not change the price you pay.
  • Clearly labelled. Affiliate links appear only in the labelled tours box, open in a new tab and are marked rel="sponsored" for search engines. Other links on this site (to campsites, tjalda.is, municipalities and so on) are not affiliate links.
  • Campsite facts are not affected. Which campsites we list and what we say about them comes only from the sources cited on each page. Commissions never change that.
  • We do not review the tours. We link to tour categories that fit the area, using the titles shown on the partner's site. We have not taken these tours and make no claims about their prices, ratings, availability or winter schedules. Check the details with the operator before booking.

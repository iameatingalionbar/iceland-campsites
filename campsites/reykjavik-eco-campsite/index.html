Reykjavík Eco-Campsite sits in Laugardalur park, next to Laugardalslaug – which the campsite describes as Iceland's biggest geothermal pool – and next door to Dalur HI Hostel. It is open all year and is about 2 km from the city centre; city bus #14 stops in front. In winter (15 October – 15 April) only Area A is open, and only to motorhomes and campervans. There is no dump station on site: the campsite sends guests to the city's disposal point at Klettagarðar 14, about a 5-minute drive away.[1][3][2]

Practical tips

  • Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 11:00. Pre-book online: you get a 4-digit access code by email that opens the gate and the facilities. Late arrival is fine if you have booked.[1][5] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Maximum stay is 7 nights in any 30-day period.[1] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Bookings are non-refundable. The campsite may move your dates within 6 months, if it has space.[1] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Rental van with no licence plate yet? Book with 'TBA' as the plate and email the plate number later.[1] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Generators, campfires and awnings, canopies or tents in Area A are not allowed. Quiet hours start at midnight. Pets are allowed if you clean up after them.[5][1] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Groceries: Krambúðin is about a 5-minute walk away and Krónan about 15 minutes. Gas canisters: N1 is about a 10-minute walk away. There is no shop on site.[1] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Small padlocked lockers with a power socket are provided for valuables. Luggage storage costs €1.50/hour or €10/day after the day you arrive (free until check-in on arrival day).[1][5] (checked 2026-10-23)

A large riverside campsite on the banks of Litla Laxá in Flúðir (Hrunamannahreppur), about 100 km from the Reykjavík area, run from the Camping Centre (Tjaldmiðstöðin). It has service buildings with toilets and showers, laundry, free Wi-Fi in the service building, water and electricity across most of the site, outdoor barbecues and a playground, and the village's shop, restaurants and swimming pool are within walking distance. It is a summer campsite: the operator's own season page says May–September open and November–April closed, so plan other options for winter.[4][1][9][2][3]

Practical tips

  • Age limit: 25 years unless accompanied by a guardian (operator's Icelandic page and price notice; tjalda.is; ferdalag.is). Children under 12 must be supervised by parents or guardians.[5][10][2][3] (checked 2026-11-09)
  • No pre-booking: the operator says the site has no marked zones and spots cannot be reserved, but it is large and usually has space (its English FAQ says plenty of space on non-holiday weekends). The Commerce Day weekend is the exception: it is much busier and only week passes are sold.[1][5][9] (checked 2026-11-09)
  • Pay on arrival at the Camping Centre (the red house at the top of the site; ferdalag.is). It sells ice cream, soft drinks, charcoal and basic camping supplies, gas cylinders and refills, and has brochures about South Iceland (tjalda.is description).[9][3][2] (checked 2026-11-09)
  • Rules (operator): speed limit 15 km/h on the site; unnecessary traffic and noise not allowed after 24:00; pets must be on a leash; travel-toilet discharge only in designated areas; breaking the rules can mean expulsion. tjalda.is's rules text also says vehicle traffic is not permitted 23:00–09:00 and quiet hours start at midnight.[10][2] (checked 2026-11-09)
  • Nearby (own site's activities links and ferdalag.is): Flúðir swimming pool, Secret Lagoon, Kaffi Sel and golf course, Farmers Bistro, Minilik Ethiopian restaurant, Litla Melabúðin shop, football floor, guided walks in Hrunamannahreppur; Gullfoss and Geysir are a short drive.[1][3] (checked 2026-11-09)
  • Long-term pitches: the operator takes long-term enquiries (one month or more for caravans or mobile homes) through its contact form and has separate rules for them.[1][10] (checked 2026-11-09)

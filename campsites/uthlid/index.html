A family campsite on the hillside below Réttin restaurant in Biskupstungur, in the middle of the Golden Circle about 10 km from Geysir and 20 km from Gullfoss (ferdalag.is: about an hour's drive from Reykjavík and 25 minutes from Þingvellir). It is open all year: in winter, motorhomes and campervans park in the area around Réttin and use the toilets in the swimming-pool building. The camping fee includes showers, the hot tubs and an indoor area for eating and washing up. Úthlíð also has holiday cottages, a 9-hole golf course, horse rental, a small shop in Réttin and a petrol station.[3][1][2][4]

Practical tips

  • Families only: the age limit is 25 unless travelling with adults.[1] (checked 2026-11-05)
  • Drop-in only, no online booking (not bookable with Parka); groups must book in advance by phone (+354 699 5500).[1][3] (checked 2026-11-05)
  • Réttin restaurant is also the reception and tourist information point. Published hours: lunch buffet 12:00–13:00; Sunday–Friday 17:00–21:00 (food can be ordered by phone, 699 5500); Saturdays 11:00–22:00. The page gives no season, so winter hours are Unconfirmed.[5] (checked 2026-11-05)
  • A small shop in Réttin sells basics such as dairy products, bread, coffee, biscuits, sweets and ice cream, plus gas for the grill and batteries.[4] (checked 2026-11-05)
  • Hot tubs are included in the camping fee; the swimming pool's own opening hours are not published on the operator's site (Unconfirmed).[3][1] (checked 2026-11-05)
  • Quiet from 23:00; personal sound systems are discouraged.[1][3] (checked 2026-11-05)
  • The 9-hole golf course (par 35) is open all summer, and only for club members outside that period.[7] (checked 2026-11-05)

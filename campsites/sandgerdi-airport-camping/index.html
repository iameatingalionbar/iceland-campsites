Airport Camping – Sandgerði

A small, central campsite on Byggðavegur in the fishing village of Sandgerði on the Reykjanes Peninsula (a UNESCO Global Geopark). The operator puts it 8 minutes' drive from Keflavík International Airport, about 24–25 minutes from the Blue Lagoon and 35 minutes from the Reykjavík area, which makes it a practical first or last night with a campervan. The service house has toilets, showers, outdoor sinks with hot and cold water, a washer and dryer; there is electricity for RVs, water refill, black and grey water disposal, free Wi-Fi and a playground. Small cabins for two can also be rented on site.[1][2][6][7]

Practical tips

  • Tjalda.is says reservations are not possible except for large groups, so individual campers just turn up. Parka.is shows a pitch-booking page, but it currently says online booking for this campsite has not been activated.[1][3] (checked 2026-10-26)
  • Parka.is says a night runs from 13:00 to 12:00 the next day.[3] (checked 2026-10-26)
  • Grocery store, ATM, post box, museum, gym, swimming pool and sauna are within walking distance, according to the operator.[1][7] (checked 2026-10-26)
  • Children 14 and under stay free; adults are counted from age 15.[1] (checked 2026-10-26)

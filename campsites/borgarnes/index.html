The town campsite of Borgarnes, at Granastaðir on Borgarbraut, right off Route 1 (the Ring Road) on the way north out of town. Grass pitches for tents and motorhomes look out over the Borgarfjörður fjord, and the operator pitches it as a base for exploring West Iceland and the Snæfellsnes peninsula. Shops, a petrol station, the swimming pool, restaurants and cafés are within walking distance. The campsite belongs to the municipality of Borgarbyggð and is run by a tenant, ÁS rekstur og þjónusta ehf.; the contact person is Ámundi Sigurðsson.[1][3][2][8][9]

Practical tips

  • Drop-in only: tjalda.is lists the campsite as accepting drop-in guests but not pre-booked stays, and payment is made on site. The 'Reserve a pitch' buttons on the operator's website only jump to its contact details. For larger groups or longer stays, the operator asks you to request a special offer by email.[3][1] (checked 2026-11-21)
  • Age limit: guests under 20 must be with a guardian (tjalda.is; ferdalag.is).[3][2] (checked 2026-11-21)
  • Dogs are allowed (ferdalag.is).[2] (checked 2026-11-21)
  • Nearby (ferdalag.is distances): petrol station and pharmacy about 0.5 km, grocery store and health centre about 0.8 km, swimming pool, bakery and bank about 1 km, restaurant, café and post office about 1.5 km.[2] (checked 2026-11-21)
  • Sights the operator suggests: the Settlement Center and Safnahús Borgarfjarðar museums, the Brákin monument and Borg á Mýrum in and around town; Eldborg crater, Hafnarfjall, Glanni waterfall and Deildartunguhver hot spring; Borgarnes swimming pool and the Hreppslaug geothermal pool.[1] (checked 2026-11-21)

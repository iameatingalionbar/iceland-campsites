The town campsite of Blönduós, in Brautarhvammur right by Route 1 (the Ring Road): coming from the south, it is on the right immediately after you cross the bridge over the river Blanda heading north. The site is fairly flat and grassy, sheltered in its upper part, and watched by security cameras. A service house has toilets (including an accessible toilet), hot and cold water and dishwashing facilities, and there are electric hook-ups. The campsite belongs to the municipality of Húnabyggð, which leased its town campsites to L&E ehf. on a short-term contract until the end of October 2026, and which has decided to move the campsite to a new location (see below).[1][4][3][2][7][12]

Practical tips

  • The campsite is moving. On 27 November 2025 Húnabyggð's council confirmed its intention to move the Blönduós campsite to a new location, and approved a plan change that turns area 2 of Brautarhvammur, previously for motorhomes and caravans, into plots for five guesthouses and a service house. A new campsite at Hnjúkabyggð is being planned (plan description dated 18 September 2026). Check which site is operating before a 2027 visit.[12][13] (checked 2026-11-22)
  • In summer 2026 the same operator also ran a temporary campsite at Hnjúkabyggð in Blönduós (10 July – 30 September): toilets and electricity (8 hook-ups in each of areas A and B), no showers yet (guests are referred to the town pool); 2026 rates adults 1,500 ISK, children free, electricity 1,500 ISK, lodging tax 400 ISK. The municipality got a permit for a 20-foot container as toilets at its planned campsite.[11][4][7] (checked 2026-11-22)
  • Booking: tjalda.is shows the campsite as taking both drop-in and pre-booked guests and links to Parka.is for reservations, but Parka.is says online booking for this campsite has not been activated. Expect to turn up and pay on site, or phone ahead.[4][3] (checked 2026-11-22)
  • Blönduós swimming pool (Sundlaugin Blönduósi, Melabraut 2, +354 455 4780) is listed as open all year; opening hours and prices are on imb.is.[14] (checked 2026-11-22)
  • East of Blönduós, Route 1 crosses the Vatnsskarð pass towards Varmahlíð, one of the Ring Road mountain passes prone to closure. Check road conditions on umferdin.is or call the traffic service on 1777 before driving.[15][16] (checked 2026-11-22)

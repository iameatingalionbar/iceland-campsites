Also known as: Tjaldsvæðið Laugarvatni, Laugarvatn Campsite, Vinastræti camping, Hotel Laugarvatn Camping

Facts last checked 2026-11-08. Numbers in brackets link to the source; full list under Sources.

A spacious village campsite in Laugarvatn on the Golden Circle, run alongside Vinastræti restaurant (ferdalag.is and Visit Iceland give the address as Háholt 2c; tjalda.is and the Camping Card page say Dalbraut). Shops and restaurants are close by, showers are included, and the site is on the 2026 Camping Card. Every published season ends by 1 October, so it is closed in winter. Hotel Laugarvatn at Dalbraut 10 is a separate hotel that appears on the official camper winter service list but does not describe a campsite — see Winter below.[1][3][4][6][8][10]

Practical tips

  • Directions from tjalda.is: when entering Laugarvatn from the direction of Reykjavík, drive through the village and turn left just before exiting.[4] (checked 2026-11-08)
  • Age limit: ferdalag.is and Visit Iceland say 18 years except when accompanied by an adult. tjalda.is's short description also says under-18s must be with a parent or guardian, but its longer about-text says the age limit is 20 years.[1][3][4] (checked 2026-11-08)
  • Quiet after 24:00 (ferdalag.is, Visit Iceland and tjalda.is).[1][3][4] (checked 2026-11-08)
  • The Camping Card page says all campsite guests receive a 20% discount on food at Vinastræti restaurant, and that a new restaurant has opened directly across from it. Vinastræti's own home page (a family-run restaurant, Vinastræti veitingahús ehf.) lists opening hours Monday–Sunday 11:30–20:30; whether those hours apply in the camping off-season is Unconfirmed.[6][12] (checked 2026-11-08)
  • Nearby according to ferdalag.is: restaurant 0.1 km, swimming pool 0.5 km, golf course 4 km; also a playground, sports field, walking path, horse riding and fishing permits. The Camping Card page (78 km from Reykjavík) said a new playground, updated restrooms and a communal barbecue were planned 'this summer'.[1][2][6] (checked 2026-11-08)
  • tjalda.is hides this listing from its public 2026 directory (show_campsite_on_tjalda is false) and still shows 2023 season wording and 2023/2024 prices, so prefer the ferdalag.is / Visit Iceland and Camping Card details for current opening dates and contact. Coordinates on this page (64.222301, −20.732167) come from that tjalda.is API listing.[4] (checked 2026-11-08)
  • Do not confuse this campsite with Hotel Laugarvatn at Dalbraut 10 (+354 899 5409, stay@hotellaugarvatn.is / info@hotellaugarvatn.is), a separate hotel whose own site and ferdalag.is page describe rooms, not camping.[10][9][8] (checked 2026-11-08)

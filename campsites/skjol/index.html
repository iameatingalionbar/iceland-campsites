Facts last checked 2026-11-06. Numbers in brackets link to the source; full list under Sources.

A large grass campsite on the farm Kjóastaðir on road 35, right between Geysir (about 3.5 km) and Gullfoss (about 6 km) on the Golden Circle. Skjól Center also runs a bistro and summer activities (buggy tours, horseback riding, e-bike rental). Check-in is at the bistro; no campsite reservations. Sources disagree on whether it is open in winter — see Winter below before relying on it outside the Camping Card season.[1][6][7][3]

Practical tips

  • No campsite reservations – drop in and check in at the bistro.[1][2] (checked 2026-11-06)
  • Skjól Bistro hours: home page says open every day 10:00–20:00; the site footer on several pages says 'Kitchen · 12:00–20:00'. Winter bistro hours beyond those published figures: Unconfirmed.[6][2] (checked 2026-11-06)
  • Distances from the Camping Card page: Geysir about 3.5 km, Gullfoss about 6 km, Brúarhlöðir about 4 km; grocery stores within about 25 km; about 110 km from Reykjavík.[7] (checked 2026-11-06)
  • Flat grassy area for tents and vans (own campsite page). One ferdalag.is entry describes about two hectares with space for hundreds of campers and caravans.[1][3] (checked 2026-11-06)
  • Summer activities from the site include buggy rides, the 'Golden Ride' horseback tour and e-bike rental (own home and ferdalag.is).[6][3] (checked 2026-11-06)

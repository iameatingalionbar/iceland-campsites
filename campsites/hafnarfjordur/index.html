Short entry. This page covers the basics we could source so far. Anything we have not been able to confirm is marked Unconfirmed.

A small, family-friendly campsite on Víðistaðatún, a sculpture park surrounded by lava in Hafnarfjörður, about 5 minutes from the town centre. It is run by the Hraunbúar scout association, and profits go to youth scouting.[1][2]

Practical tips

  • Maximum vehicle length on pitches in Zones B and C is 8 metres. Zone A is for tents only; cars with rooftop tents must book Zone B or C.[2] (checked 2026-10-31)
  • Maximum stay 7 nights. One motorhome, caravan or trailer per pitch. Dogs allowed on a lead. American Express is not accepted.[1][2] (checked 2026-10-31)
  • Pitches can be booked online through Reisa.is; drop-in guests are also accepted.[2][1] (checked 2026-10-31)

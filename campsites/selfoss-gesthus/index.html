A family-run campsite and RV park tucked among tall trees in the centre of Selfoss, the largest town in South Iceland. It is open all year, needs no booking for individual travellers, and has a heated service centre with a kitchen, a big dining room and hot showers, plus 40 electric hook-up pitches and a chemical toilet disposal point.[4][1][5]

Practical tips

  • No reservations needed for individuals. Register and pay at the service centre before setting up; if staff have to collect payment at your pitch, an extra service fee applies.[1][5] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Arriving after the service centre closes is fine: set up and pay the next morning. Check-out from the campsite and RV park is before 12:00.[5] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Quiet hours are 00:00–07:00, with no unnecessary vehicle traffic in that window. Open fires are not allowed.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Breakfast is sold in the service centre 08:00–10:00 every day, along with coffee, soft drinks, beer and wine.[2][1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • About 5 minutes' walk to the swimming pool and a large playground, and about 10 minutes to the town centre with supermarkets, restaurants and the post office.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Tents and motorhomes/folding campers use two separate areas; the electric pitches are in the RV area.[2] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Tap water is drinkable: let the cold tap run a moment so it isn't mixed with the sulphur-scented geothermal hot water.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)

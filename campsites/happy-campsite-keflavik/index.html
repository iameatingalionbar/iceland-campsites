Short entry. This page covers the basics we could source so far. Anything we have not been able to confirm is marked Unconfirmed.

A small campsite next to the Happy Campers rental office in Reykjanesbær, which the operator puts 6 minutes from Keflavík Airport.[1][2]

Practical tips

  • Late-night arrival is possible if you pre-booked on the Parka app.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Zones A and C are for campervans without electricity; zone B is for vehicles that need power.[1][2] (checked 2026-10-24)

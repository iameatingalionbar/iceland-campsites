The town campsite of Stykkishólmur, on the north side of the Snæfellsnes peninsula in West Iceland. It lies inside the town next to the 9-hole Víkurvöllur golf course, the sports field, the swimming pool and the primary school, and the shops, bakery, restaurants and fuel station are within walking distance (about 5 minutes to the pool). The town owns the site and the Mostri golf club runs it, with the reception in the golf clubhouse. Stykkishólmur is the port for boat trips around Breiðafjörður and the ferry to Flatey, and a common base for driving around Snæfellsnes.[1][3][4][2]

Practical tips

  • No pre-booking: tjalda.is lists the site as drop-in only. In summer you pay at the golf clubhouse; when it is closed there is a card terminal on site and the operator relies on guests' honesty.[4][3][1] (checked 2026-11-12)
  • Multi-night packages make longer stays cheaper: one adult pays 2,000 ISK for one night but 3,600 ISK for three nights, plus 400 ISK lodging tax per unit per night.[6][4] (checked 2026-11-12)
  • Golf clubs can be rented for the 9-hole Víkurvöllur course next to the campsite (operator; tjalda.is).[3][4] (checked 2026-11-12)
  • Things to do named by the operator: a drive around Snæfellsnes, day trips to Flatey, the town museums, kayaking, Breiðafjörður boat trips to see the islands and birdlife, and the short walk up Súgandisey island for the view over the bay.[3][4] (checked 2026-11-12)
  • Dogs are allowed (ferdalag.is).[2] (checked 2026-11-12)

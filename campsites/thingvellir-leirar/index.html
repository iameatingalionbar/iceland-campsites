Þingvellir National Park – Leirar Campsite

Also known as: Thingvellir camping, Nyrðri-Leirar, Syðri-Leirar, Fagrabrekka, Vatnskot

Facts last checked 2026-11-07. Numbers in brackets link to the source; full list under Sources.

The national park runs four campsites around the Leirar service centre (junction of roads 36, 361 and 52): Nyrðri-Leirar and Syðri-Leirar take tents and motorhomes with electricity, while Fagrabrekka and Vatnskot are for tents only in summer. Nyrðri-Leirar stays open year-round. Guests pick their own pitch; there is no formal check-in. Pay at the Leirar service centre or on the park website (or the morning after if you arrive outside desk hours).[1][2]

Practical tips

  • Advance booking is not required but is available on the national park's website; you can also pay at the Leirar service centre. If you arrive outside desk hours, the IS page asks you to pay online or at the centre the next morning. No formal check-out; leave by the following evening.[1][2] (checked 2026-11-07)
  • Pay for three nights and the fourth is free, then every second night after that (service centre only). Groups of 10+ get 15% off adults (18–66) when paying in one transaction at the service centre; discount excludes electricity and lodging tax; group web booking is not offered (email thingvellir@thingvellir.is).[1][2] (checked 2026-11-07)
  • Service-centre hours: park IS page says summer 09:00–18:00 and winter 09:00–17:00. Ferdalag.is's winter-service entry instead gives information desk 09:00–16:00 and store/service centre at Leirar 09:00–18:00 (September–May). Prefer the park's own hours and confirm on arrival if arriving late.[2][3] (checked 2026-11-07)
  • Nyrðri-Leirar: large grass field by the visitor centre north of Þingvallavegur; gravel track with motorhome power points along the edge; service house with four toilets, two showers, picnic tables, grill, and a laundry room. Syðri-Leirar (summer): south of the road, drinking-water hose at the service house, dump point and recycling a short way north. Fagrabrekka and Vatnskot: tents only, summer.[2] (checked 2026-11-07)
  • tjalda.is says this campsite is not an active participant there in 2026, so use the park's own pages for current details. Coordinates on this page come from the tjalda.is API listing (64.279586, −21.087002).[4] (checked 2026-11-07)
  • Camping overnight is only allowed on marked campsites in the park; keep quiet 23:00–08:00. Dogs on leash. Fishing permits are sold at the service centre.[2] (checked 2026-11-07)

A family-friendly campsite in Grímsnes, South Iceland, in the middle of the Golden Circle and about an hour from Reykjavík, with pitches for tents, caravans, campervans and motorhomes. It sits beside the Hraunborgir service centre, which has a swimming pool, hot tubs, sauna, restaurant (the Village Bar), TV lounge, playground and a small shop, and there is a golf course close by. Unusually for the Golden Circle it publishes year-round opening and actively advertises winter camping, and the operator says the service centre stays open through the winter. Þingvellir, Kerið, Geysir and Gullfoss are all within reach.[4][5][3][7]

Practical tips

  • Book online on the operator's site (choose dates, adults/children and optional electricity; the total is shown before you pay). tjalda.is says the site also accepts drop-in guests.[6][4] (checked 2026-11-10)
  • In winter, call site manager Sara (+354 832 3007) before arriving – she helps campers get access to the hot tubs, showers and TV lounge.[5][6] (checked 2026-11-10)
  • Service centre: swimming pool (once named one of Iceland's 100 best pools), hot tubs, sauna, restaurant and Village Bar (homemade pizza, simple dishes), TV lounge, playground and playroom, basketball and football, and a shop selling everyday essentials, gas, sweets and ice cream. Swimwear and towels are available at reception.[5][10][3] (checked 2026-11-10)
  • The campsite was recently expanded and is sheltered, with walking trails and a viewpoint dial on the hill naming the surrounding landmarks; groups can hire 'Bryggjan', a covered area attached to the restaurant.[2][3] (checked 2026-11-10)
  • Pets on a leash, wheelchair access and EV charging are listed by tjalda.is.[4] (checked 2026-11-10)
  • Golf: Hraunborgir Golf Club (Ásgeirsvöllur) and Kiðjaberg Golf Club are both close by; a round at Hraunborgir costs 3,500 ISK and tee times are booked through GolfBox.[5] (checked 2026-11-10)

Garðskagi Campsite (lighthouses)

A low-service campsite at Garðskagi Point, on the coast between the two Garðskagi lighthouses at the north-western tip of the Reykjanes Peninsula, with an open view over Faxaflói bay and the Atlantic. The operator calls it one of the best bird-watching spots in Iceland, says whales are often seen just offshore, and points to walking trails along the coast on both sides of the point. You get toilets, hot and cold water, an outdoor washing-up sink, a dump point and electricity – but no showers, laundry or indoor space. Next door are Röstin restaurant, the Garðskagi heritage and maritime museum and the big lighthouse with its northern lights and whale exhibitions. The operator puts it 11 km from Keflavík Airport.[4][8][1][2][3]

Practical tips

  • No need to book: the operator says it does not reserve pitches for single tents or small groups, the site is large and 'there will always be a spot for you'. Pay at Röstin restaurant during its opening hours or online via Parka (parka.is/gardskagi).[1][4][2] (checked 2026-11-15)
  • Röstin restaurant (upper floor of the heritage museum, next to the campsite) is open all year. Hours differ between sources: the operator's home page says 17:00–20:30, while ferdalag.is lists Monday–Wednesday 17–21 and Thursday–Sunday 12–21. If you plan to pay in person, check on arrival or pay online.[8][4][3] (checked 2026-11-15)
  • Do not confuse it with 'Airport Camping' in Sandgerði, a different campsite: Parka.is lists Garðskagi under the name 'Airport Campsite - Garðskagi'.[2] (checked 2026-11-15)
  • Next to the campsite: the Garðskagi heritage and maritime museum and the lighthouses (built 1897 and 1944); the big lighthouse, which ferdalag.is calls the biggest in the country, with a northern lights show and a whale show; admission is included in the museum fee.[3][4] (checked 2026-11-15)
  • Dogs are allowed on a leash (tjalda.is; Parka.is).[1][2] (checked 2026-11-15)

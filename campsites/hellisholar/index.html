A countryside campsite around the Þverárvöllur 9-hole golf course and the Hellishólavatn fishing lake in Fljótshlíð, about 10 km from Hvolsvöllur. It is open all year with electric hook-ups for up to 40 caravans and motorhomes, two bathhouses, laundry, a big children's playground, a hotel, cottages and the Hygge Restaurant & Bar. In winter only area G, by the reception and restaurant, is open, and there is no shared guest kitchen. Age limit 25 unless travelling with family.[6][4][3]

Practical tips

  • Book and pay before setting up (tjalda.is says via parka.is or hellisholar.is). On 2026-10-25 the Parka.is page said online booking for this campsite had not been activated, so you may need to book via hellisholar.is or call +354 487 8360.[3][2] (checked 2026-10-25)
  • Age limit 25 years unless you travel as a family with children.[3][2] (checked 2026-10-25)
  • Security cameras cover the reception and restaurant building.[3] (checked 2026-10-25)
  • Fishing in Hellishólavatn lake and the 9-hole Þverárvöllur golf course are on site; there is also a playground with a bouncy pillow.[6][3][2] (checked 2026-10-25)
  • The site also has a 36-room hotel and 24 cottages, handy if the weather turns.[3] (checked 2026-10-25)
  • Dogs on a leash are allowed.[2] (checked 2026-10-25)

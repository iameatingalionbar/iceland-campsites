Vogar Campsite, Mývatn (Vogar Travel Service)

Facts last checked 2026-10-28. Numbers in brackets link to the source; full list under Sources.

A family-run campsite beside the Vogar guesthouse and Daddi's Pizza on the east side of Lake Mývatn, in lava fields with views of Hverfjall crater, about 5 minutes from Reykjahlíð. It is open all year and has a heated indoor kitchen and dining room, hot showers included in the price, free Wi-Fi in parts of the site, electric hook-ups in the campervan area, a black-water disposal station and a drinking-water refill hose. There are no reservations: you arrive, pick a spot and pay by QR code. Walking trails to Hverfjall and Grjótagjá start near the campsite.[1][5][6][2][3][4]

Practical tips

  • No advance bookings for the campsite: find a spot and pay on arrival by scanning the QR code at the entrance, in the facility houses or on the reception door (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay), or at the self-service kiosk. Late-night arrivals are fine.[5][6][2][3] (checked 2026-10-28)
  • Quiet hours are 23:00–06:00. The rules page says to leave before 12:00, while the FAQ says there is no specific check-out time for camping.[7][6] (checked 2026-10-28)
  • Dogs on a leash only; no open fires; no grills on grass or wooden decks; no own gas stoves inside the buildings; campers may not use the guesthouse facilities.[7][6] (checked 2026-10-28)
  • Campsite guests get 15% off menu pizzas at Daddi's Pizza next door; there is also a 24/7 honesty souvenir shop.[1][2][3] (checked 2026-10-28)
  • Nearest grocery store is Krambúðin in Reykjahlíð next to the N1 petrol station (about 5 minutes); the Vínbúðin liquor store in Reykjahlíð opens weekdays 16–18.[6][2] (checked 2026-10-28)
  • There is no playground on site; the nearest is in Reykjahlíð next to the school.[6] (checked 2026-10-28)

Short entry. This page covers the basics we could source so far. Anything we have not been able to confirm is marked Unconfirmed.

A quiet campsite in a sheltered grove in Mosfellsdalur valley, a short drive from Mosfellsbær on the edge of the capital area. Nearby are horse rental, a golf course, walking paths and Gljúfrasteinn, the museum of Nobel laureate Halldór Laxness.[1]

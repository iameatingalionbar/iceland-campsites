A small family-run campsite on a market garden and tree nursery in Mosfellsdalur valley, about 17 km from central Reykjavík on the road towards Þingvellir and the Golden Circle. The pitches are split into sections surrounded by trees, which makes the site very sheltered, and it takes tents, folding campers, caravans and motorhomes. Instead of a classic kitchen block there is a greenhouse where guests can sit indoors, cook and eat, plus a covered area with tables and benches outside.[3][1]

Practical tips

  • Drop-in only: tjalda.is says it accepts drop-in guests, takes no pre-bookings and is not bookable through Parka.[3] (checked 2026-11-14)
  • Vegetable market on the farm every Saturday, 10:00–15:00, from the first weekend in July into autumn (ferdalag.is says to the end of September; its Icelandic page says July to October).[3][1][2] (checked 2026-11-14)
  • Nearby in Mosfellsdalur, per tjalda.is: Gljúfrasteinn (the Halldór Laxness museum), horse rental at Laxnes, Bakkakot golf course, a farm-animal park at Hraðastaðir and rose and strawberry sales at Dalsgarður. Swimming pools, grocery shops and Mosfellsbakarí bakery are a short drive away in Mosfellsbær.[3] (checked 2026-11-14)
  • Handy first or last night of a Golden Circle loop: the farm sits on the road to Þingvellir National Park, about 15 minutes' drive from central Reykjavík (operator text on ferdalag.is).[1] (checked 2026-11-14)

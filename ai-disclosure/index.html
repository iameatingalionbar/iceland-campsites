AI disclosure

Content on this site is prepared with the help of AI tools: researching public sources, extracting facts, and drafting and checking pages.

Facts about campsites come only from the public sources cited on each page (campsite websites, tjalda.is, ferdalag.is, municipalities and national parks), each with the date it was checked. If a fact could not be confirmed from a source, it is marked Unconfirmed rather than filled in.

AI-assisted research can still make mistakes, and campsites change their seasons and prices. Please verify opening hours, winter access and prices with the campsite before you travel.

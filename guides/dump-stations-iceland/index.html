Where to Empty a Campervan Toilet in Iceland: Dump Stations by Region (2026)

Toilet waste from a campervan or motorhome may only be emptied at a designated disposal point, according to Iceland's Environment and Energy Agency (Umhverfis- og orkustofnun). This page lists the disposal points we could confirm from a source, with the date we checked.[1]

Most dump points are at campsites. On 24 October 2026, 58 of the 181 campsites in the tjalda.is database were tagged 'Waste disposal for mobile homes'. Treat that tag as a lead and confirm with the campsite.[2]

Scope: campsites with a page on this site, plus the official capital-area disposal points. For the rest of the country, use the official map linked below. Fuel-station dump points are listed only where a source names them.

Official disposal points in the capital area

The Environment and Energy Agency lists two portable-toilet disposal points in the capital area: Klettagarðar in Reykjavík and Hraunavík in Hafnarfjörður. Its interactive map shows the disposal points for the whole country.[1][42] (checked 2026-10-24)

Reykjavík Eco-Campsite (Laugardalur) has no disposal point on site and sends campers to Klettagarðar 14.[5] (checked 2026-10-23)

Akureyri

Visit Akureyri lists dump points at the Hamrar and Hrafnagil campsites and at the Olís fuel station in Akureyri.[43] (checked 2026-10-24)

Other lists (use with care)

icetourist.is publishes an undated region-by-region list of places where 'motor caravan tanks can be emptied'. For the capital area it names Klettagarðar 14, Óseyrarbraut 18 in Hafnarfjörður and Aðalgata 60 in Keflavík (Motel Alex). The official agency page names Hraunavík, not Óseyrarbraut, for Hafnarfjörður, so the third-party list may be out of date.[44][1] (checked 2026-10-24)

The rules, in short

  • Toilet waste may only be emptied at designated disposal points.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Never use a fresh-water hose to rinse a portable toilet or drain.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Portable toilets are only for urine, faeces and toilet paper; use toilet paper intended for portable toilets.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Use environmentally friendly, biodegradable cleaning products and avoid chlorine, formaldehyde and phosphates.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Where possible, dispose of kitchen and shower wastewater (grey water) separately from toilet waste.[1] (checked 2026-10-24)

Practical tips

  • Between Vík and Höfn, the dump points we could confirm are at Vík, Skaftafell and Höfn. Kirkjubær II has no source either way.[33][36][41] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Near Keflavík Airport, Sandgerði lists black- and grey-water disposal and water-tank refill, and Garðskagi lists a dump point. Both are listed as open all year.[10][15] (checked 2026-10-24, 2026-10-26)
  • In winter, check that the campsite itself is open before you drive there to empty your tank: Hvolsvöllur and Vík are closed in winter but have dump points.[25][33] (checked 2026-10-23)
Dump points can be closed in frost or moved. If a campsite is closed, ask before using its facilities. If in doubt, call the campsite or use the official map.

Campsites Open in Winter on the Golden Circle (2026–27)

Three campsites on or near the Golden Circle publish year-round opening for 2026–27: Nyrðri-Leirar in Þingvellir National Park, Úthlíð in Biskupstungur between Laugarvatn and Geysir, and Hraunborgir in Grímsnes.[1][2][3][4][5]

The campsites closest to Geysir, Laugarvatn and Flúðir publish summer seasons only. Geysir and Flúðir also appear on the official camper winter service list, which conflicts with their own dates. Hotel Laugarvatn is on that list too, but that entry describes the hotel at Dalbraut 10, not the village campsite.[6][7][8][9][10][11]

Scope: campsites on the Golden Circle (Þingvellir – Laugarvatn – Geysir – Gullfoss) and the nearby Grímsnes, Biskupstungur and Flúðir area, checked against operator pages, tjalda.is and ferdalag.is's camper winter service list on 2026-11-04. Hveragerði and Selfoss, which many Golden Circle drivers also use, are covered in the South Coast winter guide.

Open in winter: at a glance

Open in winterCall aheadShoulder season onlyClosed in winterUnconfirmed

Open in winter: the campsite or an official listing states it is open through winter. Call ahead: listed as open in winter, but the wording is vague or sources conflict. Shoulder season only: open into late autumn or from early spring, not all winter. Closed in winter: published season ends before winter.

Which one to pick in winter

  • You want the cheapest night: Úthlíð charges 2,000 ISK per adult including showers and hot tubs, and Þingvellir 1,800 ISK per adult (900 ISK for seniors), each plus 400 ISK lodging tax per unit. Hraunborgir charges 3,500 ISK per adult.[3][1][4] (checked 2026-11-05, 2026-11-07, 2026-11-10)
  • You want a hot tub after a cold day: Úthlíð includes its hot tubs in the camping fee, and Hraunborgir advertises winter camping with hot tubs, shower access and a TV lounge.[2][5] (checked 2026-11-05, 2026-11-10)
  • You need electricity: all three list hook-ups. At Þingvellir in winter you park in front of the visitor centre or along the campsite access road, where motorhomes can connect (1,100 ISK per 24 hours).[1][3][4] (checked 2026-11-05, 2026-11-07, 2026-11-10)
  • You need to empty a cassette toilet: Úthlíð's own page lists sludge disposal for motorhomes and a drinking-water hose, and tjalda.is lists 'waste disposal for mobile homes' at Hraunborgir. At Þingvellir, the park's Icelandic campsite page lists chemical-toilet disposal (losun ferðasalerna) at Syðri-Leirar (summer 1 June – 15 September only); winter disposal at year-round Nyrðri-Leirar is Unconfirmed.[2][4][12] (checked 2026-11-05, 2026-11-07, 2026-11-10)
  • You want laundry and showers in winter: at Þingvellir, toilets, showers and laundry stay available in winter, although the outdoor sinks are closed and parts of the campsite may close in heavy snow or thaw.[1] (checked 2026-11-07)

Golden Circle campsites that are closed in winter

These campsites publish a summer season for 2026. Some of them are on ferdalag.is's camper winter service list, which conflicts with their own dates, so do not plan a winter night there without confirming directly.[9] (checked 2026-11-08)

  • Geysir, Haukadalur: open 15 May – 15 September (own price list 2026 and tjalda.is).[6][31] (checked 2026-11-04)
  • Laugarvatn village campsite (Háholt 2c / Dalbraut): published seasons all end by 1 October (Camping Card 2026: 1 June – 1 October; ferdalag.is and Visit Iceland: 22 May – 15 September; tjalda.is hidden listing: 20 May – 1 October, Icelandic text dated 2023). Winter status is closed. Hotel Laugarvatn (Dalbraut 10) is on the ferdalag.is winter list and tagged 'Camper winter service', but its own website and ferdalag hotel page describe hotel rooms only, not a campsite. Full page: /campsites/hotel-laugarvatn-camping/.[10][16][15][7][9][11][18] (checked 2026-11-08)
  • Flúðir: the campsite's own FAQ says open 1 May – 30 September; tjalda.is says May – October. Either way it is closed in winter, although it is on the ferdalag.is winter list.[8][32][9] (checked 2026-11-04, 2026-11-08)
  • Reykholt (Aratunga): tjalda.is gives no season and shows 2025 rates; winter status Unconfirmed.[33] (checked 2026-11-04)
  • Árnes (Þjórsárdalur road): open 30 May – 15 September (tjalda.is).[34] (checked 2026-11-04)

Call ahead: Skjól between Geysir and Gullfoss

Skjól Center's own site and ferdalag.is's camper winter service list say the campsite is open all year (24/7). The 2026 Camping Card list gives 21 May – 15 September only, and tjalda.is marks Skjól as not an active 2026 participant. Winter status on this site is therefore call-ahead. Published camping fee: 2,000 ISK per adult (18+), children free, showers 500 ISK separate; no kitchen. Full page: /campsites/skjol/.[25][27][9][15][29] (checked 2026-11-06, 2026-11-08)

Practical winter tips

  • Check road conditions before every drive on umferdin.is (the road authority's map) and alerts on safetravel.is; conditions on the Golden Circle can change quickly in winter.[35][36] (checked 2026-11-04)
  • At Úthlíð in winter, motorhomes and campervans park in the area around Réttin restaurant and use the toilets in the swimming-pool building.[2][3] (checked 2026-11-05)
  • At Þingvellir, pay for three nights and the fourth is free (service centre only). Service-centre hours: park IS page says summer 09:00–18:00 and winter 09:00–17:00; ferdalag.is instead lists information desk 09:00–16:00 and store/service centre 09:00–18:00 in winter — confirm on arrival. Full page: /campsites/thingvellir-leirar/.[1][12][9] (checked 2026-11-07)
  • At Hraunborgir, the site manager helps with access to the facilities in winter (Sara, +354 832 3007).[5] (checked 2026-11-10)

Campsites Near Keflavík Airport (KEF) for Your First or Last Night (2026)

Airport Camping in Sandgerði puts itself 8 minutes from Keflavík International Airport, and Happy Campsite Keflavík says it is 6 minutes away. Both are on the Reykjanes Peninsula, together with Garðskagi and Vogar. Reykjavík Eco-Campsite is the capital-area alternative.[1][2]

In winter, Sandgerði, Garðskagi and Vogar are listed as open all year (for Sandgerði, Parka.is's dates start on 9 January, so ring ahead in deep winter). Happy Campsite Keflavík's season is 1 May – 1 October.[1][3][4][2][5]

Scope: campsites on the Reykjanes Peninsula near Keflavík Airport, plus Reykjavík Eco-Campsite. Grindavík is not included because tjalda.is said on 2026-10-24 that 2026 information from the operator is still awaited.

Which one to pick

  • You need showers and laundry: Sandgerði lists showers, washing machine and dryer, and water-tank refill.[1] (checked 2026-10-26)
  • Cheapest listed adult rate: Garðskagi at 1,700 ISK, but it is low-service: 24-hour toilets, a dump point and electricity, and no showers or indoor space.[3] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • You want a kitchen: Vogar lists a kitchen, showers and free WiFi.[11] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • You are picking up or returning a Happy Campers van: Happy Campsite is next to their office (season 1 May – 1 October). Book electricity in advance; there are only a few connections.[6][2] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • You want the city: Reykjavík Eco-Campsite is open all year (only Area A in winter), with a guest kitchen.[14][15] (checked 2026-10-23)

Practical tips

  • Sandgerði: tjalda.is says reservations are not possible except for large groups, so just turn up.[1] (checked 2026-10-26)
  • Happy Campsite: late-night arrival is possible only if you pre-booked on the Parka app.[2] (checked 2026-10-24)
  • Garðskagi: pay at Röstin restaurant during opening hours, or online at parka.is/gardskagi.[3] (checked 2026-10-24)
Seasons, prices and facilities can change at short notice. Contact the campsite before relying on it, especially in winter.

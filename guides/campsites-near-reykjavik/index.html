Campsites Near Reykjavík for Campervans (2026): City, Hafnarfjörður and Mosfellsdalur

Three campsites in the capital area publish 2026 information: Reykjavík Eco-Campsite in Laugardalur, about 3 km from the city centre; Hafnarfjörður's campsite on Víðistaðatún, about 5 minutes from the town centre; and Mosskógar, in a sheltered grove in Mosfellsdalur valley.[1][2][3]

Only Reykjavík Eco-Campsite is open all year, and from 15 October to 15 April only its Area A is open, for motorhomes and campervans. Mosskógar is open 1 March – 1 December, and Hafnarfjörður 15 May – 30 September.[4][5][3][6][2]

Scope: campsites within the capital area (Höfuðborgarsvæðið) listed on tjalda.is with 2026 information, checked 2026-10-31. The town campsite in Mosfellsbær (Skólabraut) is not included: its tjalda.is entry is hidden from the public site and still shows 2022 prices, so its 2026 status is Unconfirmed.

Which one to pick

  • You are travelling between mid-October and mid-April: Reykjavík Eco-Campsite is the only one of the three that is open, and only Area A, for motorhomes and campervans. Caravans and trailer tents are allowed only 15 May – 15 October.[4][5] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • You want an indoor kitchen: Reykjavík Eco-Campsite has a guest kitchen, and Mosskógar a kitchenette. Hafnarfjörður has no indoor kitchen, only outdoor sinks, grills and a roofed outdoor kitchen.[1][3][2] (checked 2026-10-23, 2026-10-31)
  • You need to empty a cassette toilet: Hafnarfjörður lists cassette-toilet disposal on site. Reykjavík Eco-Campsite has none and sends you to Klettagarðar 14, about 5 minutes' drive. Mosskógar lists 'waste disposal for mobile homes', but whether it takes cassettes is Unconfirmed.[2][4][3] (checked 2026-10-23, 2026-10-31)
  • Lowest listed price for two adults in one campervan without electricity: Hafnarfjörður at 3,500 + 2,500 ISK plus 400 ISK lodging tax, Mosskógar at 2 × 3,000 ISK with tax included, and Reykjavík Eco at 3,735 + 3,150 ISK plus 400 ISK tax.[6][3][7] (checked 2026-10-23, 2026-10-31)
  • You want to be in the city without driving: Reykjavík Eco-Campsite is next to Laugardalslaug pool, bus 14 stops in front, and airport and tour buses stop by the hostel next door.[1] (checked 2026-10-23)

Practical tips

  • Reykjavík Eco-Campsite: check-in is online, and the confirmation email contains a code that opens the gates to your area and the service building. Book ahead on its website.[1] (checked 2026-10-23)
  • Hafnarfjörður: vehicles on pitches in Zones B and C can be at most 8 metres long, and only Zone B has power sockets.[2] (checked 2026-10-31)
  • Hafnarfjörður and Reykjavík Eco-Campsite both have a maximum stay of 7 nights (Reykjavík: 7 nights within a month).[6][1] (checked 2026-10-23, 2026-10-31)
  • The Environment and Energy Agency lists two public portable-toilet disposal points in the capital area: Klettagarðar in Reykjavík and Hraunavík in Hafnarfjörður.[8] (checked 2026-10-23)
Seasons, prices and facilities can change at short notice. Contact the campsite before relying on it, especially in winter.

Audience: campervan/motorhome travellers in Iceland. Already have/contacted (see NOTES.md → Monetization): Go Campers / Go Car Rental,
Campervan Iceland, Camper Rental Iceland, Travel to Iceland. **Nothing below has been signed up for, no forms submitted, no emails sent.**

All terms are **stated by them, not verified** – read from each program's own page (WebFetch) on **2026-10-24** unless marked otherwise.
Numbers marked "(3rd-party, unverified)" came only from search snippets / directories and must be re-checked before relying on them.
"Fit" is for this site (English, static, Iceland campsites, campervan/motorhome readers).

## General risks that apply to everything
- **GitHub Pages usage policy** (https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits, checked 2026-10-24):
  "GitHub Pages is not intended for or allowed to be used as a free web-hosting service to run your online business, e-commerce site,
  or any other website that is primarily directed at either facilitating commercial transactions…". A content site with a few affiliate
  links is probably fine, but a site that is mostly booking widgets is a grey area. Moving to a custom domain + Cloudflare Pages/Netlify
  (see NOTES.md "Decisions") before heavy monetisation removes this risk. No program page checked here explicitly requires a custom
  domain, but manual reviewers (Guide to Iceland, Blue, Lava, CJ/Awin/Amazon) may judge a `*.github.io` site with little traffic as weak.
- Most programs pay only after the trip/rental is completed; cancellations earn nothing. Expect months between click and payout.
- Affiliate disclosure page must be filled in before links go live (World Nomads even mandates their own disclaimer text).

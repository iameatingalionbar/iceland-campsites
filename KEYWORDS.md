# Keyword research: Iceland campsites for campervans (2026-10-24)

**Method.** Manual SERP observation on 2026-10-24 (web search results only; we have no keyword tool) plus Reddit threads
already logged in NOTES.md. **No search volumes or difficulty scores are given, because we have none.** Priority is our own
judgement from four signals: (1) how often the query or its close variants returned forum or "best of" listicles from
campervan rental companies (a sign of demand nobody answers with sourced data); (2) whether official sources exist that we can cite;
(3) whether the SERP is dominated by big brands; (4) fit with our data model (campsite facts plus winter status).

**SERP observations (2026-10-24):**
- Winter-camping queries return rental-company blogs (rent.is, campeasy.com, ringroadiceland.com) and two list competitors
  (mapoficeland.is winter list of 23 sites; campersearch.is winter list). Most have no per-fact sources or "last checked" dates. This is our edge.
- Dump-station queries return the agency page (uos.is), an undated icetourist.is list, Visit Akureyri, and rental blogs.
  We found no maintained, sourced, region-by-region page.
- Airport and Golden Circle queries return rental blogs and tjalda.is/parka.is listings. Official sources exist (tjalda, operators, thingvellir.is).
- "Camping card" queries are dominated by utilegukortid.is and rental blogs. We can only win with an honest worth-it calculator.

Priority: **P1** = build next (clear demand plus citable data), **P2** = soon, **P3** = later/long tail.
Backlog IDs refer to NOTES.md.
